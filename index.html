Part 6 · The commercial & procurement path

The ‘how’ that follows — built for speed and quality, not lowest cost.

This comes after the ambition, not before it. Fewer, deeper partners on the PSL and an MSA renewal that sets the terms — all in service of moving faster, with quality built in.

Speed and quality first  Even where a conversation starts with price, this model exists to sustain pace and protect quality. Cost discipline follows — it doesn’t lead.

Fewer partners, more momentum

One partner, one operating model across Data, Engineering and AI for DT & Ops — less coordination drag, deeper context and faster decisions. Consolidating onto the PSL supports it.

Anchors on the MSA renewal

The renewal is the moment to set the partnership terms: a capacity / pod construct, outcome-linked incentives, IP & data terms, governance cadence and per-tier exit criteria — rather than renewing the old T&M terms and adding partnership on later.

Capacity that keeps pace

Standing, pre-approved capacity ready when the roadmap moves, so delivery never waits on a new SoW. Predictable enough to plan, fast enough to mobilise — momentum is the point.

Rewards speed and quality

Incentives track the outcomes that matter — speed to market, quality, yield — with clear scope and exit criteria so pace never costs control. Cost discipline follows the value, it doesn’t lead.

Part 7 · A delivery model we’d propose

Forward Deployed Engineering — a proven form of the ArgoPod model.

A joint pod that builds the real thing inside argenx’s systems and leaves capability behind — outcome-accountable, working within DT’s governance and architecture. It scales in three tiers, each earning the next.

Our suggested approach to building the platform together — and to the Delivery dimension in Part 5.
01 — FRAME
“Is this worth doing?”
Duration  3–4 weeks
Pod  Strategy · Technical · Experience leads
Ships: prioritised use cases, an ROI hypothesis and a scoped Sprint proposal.
Exit: reframe or stop — no bad build.
02 — SPRINT
“Does it work in production?”
Duration  6–8 weeks
Pod  AI / Data lead + engineer(s)
Ships: one workflow live in production, real users from week 3, eval from day one.
Exit: rebuild the architecture, or fund Embed.
03 — EMBED
“Can it run at full load?”
Duration  3–6 months
Pod  AI / Data + Cloud + CX pod
Ships: production at scale, enterprise integration, adoption metrics.
Exit: argenx runs the next use case itself.

How it fits the ArgoPod model

Frame = the inception pod (validate PoC / PoV).
Sprint + Embed = the delivery argopods.
Earned continuation = a long-lived team, with capability transferred to argenx — not dependency created.

Where we’ve done this — Stryker

Full embedded FDE — building AI systems inside Stryker’s own environment, with a co-build pod and progressive handover. A regulated medtech setting close to argenx’s, with capability left in the client’s hands.
